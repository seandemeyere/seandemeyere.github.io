About the game

Jolly Jars is a relaxing sort puzzle with a festive twist. Tap a jar, tap another, and move the treats across until every jar holds just one kind. It's simple to learn, gently challenging and made for unwinding with a cup of cocoa.

A 24-door advent calendar brings a special puzzle each day from December 1 to Christmas Eve, and every puzzle you solve fills a cozy living room with Christmas cheer: trim the tree, hang stockings by the fireplace, light up the window and make the cozy corner your own, with Beans the cat curled up by the fire. Collect every decoration to reveal a secret final surprise.

Features

  • A daily advent door from December 1 to 24, with catch-up for missed days
  • 24 decorations to collect for a cozy Christmas room
  • Four themes: Christmas trinkets, the Cocoa Cafe (liquid pours), the Cookie Jar and the Candy Shop
  • No timers; boss levels add a move limit and hidden items for an extra challenge
  • Up to three stars per level, with replays to improve
  • Boosters: Undo, Extra Jar, Shuffle and Solve Check
  • Plays offline, with festive music and soft, cozy sounds

About Sunshine and Storm

Sunshine and Storm is a new indie studio from Canada, making cozy, playful games for every kind of day. It's a small family effort, and Jolly Jars is its first game. More are on the way, including Sweet Slide, a shoot-and-merge drinks game.
