Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Sunshine and Storm ("we", "us") handles information in our mobile and web games (the "Games"), including Jolly Jars. Sunshine and Storm is based in Canada.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location through the Games.

Your game progress, settings and in-game coins are stored on your device. If you delete the Game, this data is deleted with it.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you.

Advertising

Our free Games show ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type and settings, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information according to its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads you can choose to watch in exchange for in-game items.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation of what was bought so we can deliver it in the Game.

Your choices

  • Personalized ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).
  • iOS tracking: On iOS, we ask for your permission before any tracking across other companies' apps and websites. You can say no, and you'll still see non-personalized ads.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Where required, the Games show a consent message before personalized ads are served, and you can change your choice later from the Game's settings.

Children

Our Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, then delete them. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. Email us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our Games change. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.
